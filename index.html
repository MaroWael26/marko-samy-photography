Behind the Lens

Meet Marko Samy

With a career spanning over 15 years since 2008, I've had the privilege of capturing thousands of stories across Egypt and beyond. From the bustling streets of Cairo to the serene shores of the Red Sea, my lens has documented the full spectrum of human emotion and beauty.

In 2025, I made the bold decision to relocate to the United States and plant new roots in New Jersey. This move represents not just a change in geography, but a renewed commitment to my craft — bringing an international eye and a fresh perspective to every project.

"Photography is not just about capturing what you see — it's about preserving what you feel."
